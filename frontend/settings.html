ℹ️ Support & Information

❓ Help & FAQ

Find answers to common questions about TruthLens.

What is TruthLens?

TruthLens is an AI-powered news verification tool that analyzes news text and links and provides a model-based classification.

How do I check a news article?

Go to the Home page, choose News Text or News Link, enter the information, and click Check News.

Is the result always correct?

No. TruthLens provides an AI/ML classification and should not be treated as absolute proof of whether information is true or false.

Can I change my profile information?

Yes. Use Edit Profile in the Settings page to update your name and email information.

Can I use TruthLens as a guest?

Yes. Select Continue as Guest on the Login page to use TruthLens without signing in.

🛡️ About TruthLens

Learn more about TruthLens and how it helps you evaluate information.

What is TruthLens?

TruthLens is an AI-powered news analysis platform designed to help users examine news articles, messages, and claims.

How does it work?

TruthLens analyzes the information you provide using a machine-learning model and returns a classification together with a confidence value.

What does the result mean?

The result represents the model's classification of the submitted information. It should be used as an aid for further verification rather than as absolute proof.

Our goal

TruthLens aims to make information checking simple, accessible, and easy to understand.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains how TruthLens handles information when you use the application.

Information You Provide

Information you enter into TruthLens, such as news text, links, and profile details, may be used by the application to provide its features.

Profile Information

Your name and email information used by the current frontend login system are stored locally in your browser.

Local Storage

TruthLens uses browser local storage to remember settings such as your theme, language, notification preferences, and current profile information.

News Analysis

News information submitted for analysis may be sent to the TruthLens backend so that the machine-learning model can process it and return a result.

Important Notice

TruthLens provides machine-learning based analysis. Results should not be considered absolute proof of whether information is true or false.

Privacy Updates

This privacy information may be updated as TruthLens develops new features and functionality.
